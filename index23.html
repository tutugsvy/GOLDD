distribution experiment

Hold the frog. Mine the token.

$SHIFROGS launches with a supply of 1 billion tokens, with 6.67% permanently burned upon minting. This NFT collection transforms the distribution process into a daily ritual: every frog NFT holder continuously mines a certain amount of $SHIFROGS directly to their wallet.

Every trade carries a fee. Half is used to buy back and burn $SHIFROGS from the market — shrinking supply. The other half buys tokenized NVDA stock and streams it straight to frog holders. The harder the community trades, the rarer the token becomes and the more NVIDIA the frogs own.

The mint is not only a PFP drop. It is a strange little machine for turning attention, holding, and participation into token flow.
