        how the frog mining rig works

        Every Frog participates.

        The Frog Mining Rig operates continuously in fifteen-minute cycles. At the end of every cycle, the rig seals a new block and selects one Frog from the complete 3,333-Frog collection.

        Every Frog participates, whether it has already been minted or is still waiting inside the collection. When a Frog mines a block, the block's $SHIFROGS reward becomes attached to that Frog.

        If the winning Frog already has an owner, its unpaid reward becomes eligible for distribution to the current owner. If the Frog is still unminted, the reward remains locked inside that Frog until someone mints it.

        Unpaid rewards travel with the Frog. The current owner at the time of payout receives the unpaid balance. Rewards that have already been paid do not transfer with future ownership.

        important rule

        No browser-side winners.

        The countdown uses the server's next block close timestamp. The website never selects a winner locally and never creates new $SHIFROGS tokens. It records distribution from an existing finite mining reserve.

        Exact "Mint Frog #____" buttons are only shown when exact minting support is configured. Otherwise the page shows a general mint route and warns that random minting cannot guarantee a reward-bearing Frog.
